Frequently Asked Questions & Guide

Learn how PulseGuard protects your privacy, calculates plain-English insights, and processes data.

How is my financial data stored and kept private?
100% of your transactions, categories, and monthly budgets are stored locally inside your browser using the HTML5 LocalStorage API. No data is ever transmitted to cloud servers, remote databases, or third-party analytical platforms. You can export complete JSON backups anytime for safekeeping.
What is the recommended 50/30/20 budget framework?
The 50/30/20 rule divides your after-tax income into three buckets: 50% for Needs (Housing, Groceries, Utilities, Healthcare), 30% for Wants (Dining, Entertainment, Shopping), and 20% for Savings or Debt Paydown. The dashboard insights engine automatically flags when your savings rate drops below or exceeds the healthy 20% target.
What CSV column formats are supported for bank imports?
The importer accepts standard RFC-4180 CSV files. Supported header variants include: Date (YYYY-MM-DD or MM/DD/YYYY), Type (expense/income), Category, Description (or Payee), Amount (with or without currency symbols), and Payment Method. You can download our sample CSV template from the CSV & Backup tab to verify formatting.
How are the plain-English financial insights generated?
The built-in analytics engine evaluates your spending in real time. It checks for category budget overages (e.g. “Dining is 32% over budget”), nearing thresholds (>85% spent), month-over-month category variance (e.g. “Groceries decreased by 12%”), and top expense concentration drivers.
Can I switch currencies and print financial statements?
Yes. You can switch between USD ($), EUR (€), GBP (£), INR (&inr;), CAD (C$), AUD (A$), and JPY (¥) anytime from the top navigation. Clicking “Print Report” opens a printer-optimized monthly statement layout hiding all navigation chrome and buttons.

Privacy Policy & LocalStorage Terms

1. Zero Cloud Data Collection: PulseGuard does not transmit your personal financial records, income figures, spending history, or category limits to any external server. 100% of all operations occur client-side inside your browser sandbox and embedded SQLite database.

2. LocalStorage Persistence: All transactions are saved in your device's browser storage under secure domain-isolated storage keys. You have complete ownership of your data and can export or wipe it at any time.
